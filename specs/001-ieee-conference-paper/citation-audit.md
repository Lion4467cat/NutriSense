**Scope**: All in-text citations in `paper/main.tex`, every entry in `paper/references.bib`, the metadata shown by the compiled IEEE bibliography, and whether each cited source supports its nearby claim. ## Corrections made in this audit

- Replaced the MoGe-2 arXiv preprint citation with the final NeurIPS 2025 proceedings record and DOI.
- Updated the Meta SAM 2 software citation to the canonical project title and clarified that it is a repository citation for official code/checkpoints.
- Reworded the 9–10 compliance threshold sentence. The Karnataka documents establish the state extension and matching ingredient quantities, but do not specify a separate IX–X kcal/protein norm. The manuscript now explicitly describes 700 kcal / 20 g as NutriSense's application mapping from those quantities to the central upper-primary reference.
- Qualified the WED bibliography page span: the author's PDF confirms a nine-page paper, while the page-range metadata is not printed as a volume-wide range in that primary PDF.

## Citation-key reconciliation

All ten bibliography keys are cited in `paper/main.tex`; each citation resolves to one record, and no record is unused. The manuscript does not include the NutriSense GitHub repository as a bibliography item. The fresh `paper/build.sh` run produced a five-page PDF and IEEE bibliography with no undefined-citation or overfull-box warnings. The only LaTeX notices were the existing float-only page and underfull vertical-box notices for the full-width flowchart.

## Claim boundaries retained

- ArUco's cited occlusion handling is not attributed to NutriSense's single-marker setup.
- MoGe-2 supports metric-scale point-map prediction; marker calibration and fallback behavior are implementation-specific.
- SigLIP 2's published benchmark results do not imply independent NutriSense recognition accuracy.
- Synthetic geometry/depth values, gallery leave-one-out counts, and WED DEV figures are author-generated results and retain their evaluation scope in the manuscript.
- WED evidence supports its dataset/annotation description, not the still-pending NutriSense real-meal field dataset.
- National PM POSHAN coverage ends at class VIII. Karnataka separately documents IX–X extension; the 9–10 calorie/protein cutoff is explicitly labeled an application mapping from upper-primary quantities, not an official Karnataka grade-specific norm.
- The classifier returns one selected dish per capture, so the policy category is not a whole-meal compliance decision for a multi-dish plate.
